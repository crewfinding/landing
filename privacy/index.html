1. Who we are

[LEGAL ENTITY NAME] ("CrewFinding", "we") operates the CrewFinding mobile application and website. We are based in Montreal, Quebec, Canada. Our designated privacy officer under Quebec Law 25 is [NAME, EMAIL].

2. What CrewFinding does with data, in one paragraph

CrewFinding is a work tool. A company (your employer or contractor) creates jobs; crew members use the app on those jobs. During a work session the app records location, work hours, photos, signatures and job expenses so the company can run its jobs, pay its crews and keep its records. The company you work for or hired is responsible for the job data in its account; we process it on their behalf.

3. Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email, phone number, role, company.
  • Location data: GPS position of crew members during active work sessions only, including geofenced arrival and departure stamps. Location is not collected when a worker is clocked out. [CONFIRM APP BEHAVIOR]
  • Time and work data: clock-in/clock-out times, job assignments, hours per job.
  • Documents and signatures: contracts, bills of lading, waivers and invoices signed in the app, with timestamps, including client signatures.
  • Photos and receipts: images taken in the app and attached to jobs (delivery proof, expense receipts).
  • Payment data: processed by our payment processor [PROCESSOR NAME]; we do not store full card numbers.
  • Device and usage data: device type, app version, crash logs, and basic analytics.

4. Why we collect it

  • To provide the service: dispatching, tracking, timekeeping, timesheet totals, document filing, payment collection.
  • To let the account-holding company see its jobs, hours, costs and records.
  • To secure accounts, prevent fraud and meet legal obligations.
  • To improve the product using aggregated, de-identified usage data.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use crew location data for advertising.

5. Who can see your information

  • Your company: the company that added you to its CrewFinding account sees your work data (location during sessions, hours, job activity, documents).
  • Clients: a client sees the documents they signed and their own receipts.
  • Service providers: hosting ([HOST]), payments ([PROCESSOR]), analytics ([TOOLS]), bound by contract to protect the data.
  • Legal: when required by law or to protect rights and safety.
