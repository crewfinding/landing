The honest questions

Asked by owners like you.

What does CrewFinding cost?

Plans start at $40 a month and are priced by truck: $40 for up to 2 trucks, $60 for 3 trucks and $80 for up to 5 trucks. For more than 5 trucks, contact us. Prices are in Canadian dollars. Everyone on your crews is included and every feature is included. There are no per-seat fees, ever.

Will my crews hate being tracked?

Transparent hours mean your crew is paid for every minute they work. Good crews prefer it: no more arguing about when the day started or ended.

I already use spreadsheets. Why switch?

The spreadsheet cannot see the truck arrive, collect the signature at the door, or take the payment. CrewFinding does all three in the field, and the Sunday-night spreadsheet work disappears.

Is this just another monthly software bill?

One mispriced job costs more than a year of CrewFinding. At $40 to $80 a month with everything included, the first job it saves pays for the year.
