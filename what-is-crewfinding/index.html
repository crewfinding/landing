What is CrewFinding?

You just signed something on CrewFinding. Here's what that was.

A crew showed up, handed you a phone, and you signed. Good news: that signature protects you as much as it protects them.

✍️What just happened

The company doing your job runs its crews on CrewFinding. When you signed on their phone, your contract, bill of lading or waiver was timestamped and saved instantly to your job's file. Nothing to print, nothing that can be lost in a truck.

🔒Is it legitimate?

Yes. Your signature is captured with the date, the time and the job it belongs to, and the record cannot be edited after the fact. Both you and the company are protected by the same document. If you want a copy, ask the company that did your job: it is one tap for them to send it.

📍Why the crew uses this app

CrewFinding is how small service companies (movers, delivery teams, landscapers, contractors) run their day: GPS dispatch, tracked hours, signed paperwork and payment, all from the crew's phone. It means faster jobs and fewer arguments, for them and for you.

💳If you paid through the app

Your payment went directly to the company, not to the crew member's pocket, and your receipt lives in the same job file as your signed documents.
