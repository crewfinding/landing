Questions moving companies ask

Before you download it.

Does CrewFinding work for small moving companies?

That is exactly who it is built for: moving companies running two to five crews. It is priced by truck, from $40 a month, with everyone included.

Can clients sign the bill of lading on a phone?

Yes. The client signs the contract, BOL and damage waiver on your crew's phone at the door. Every signed document saves itself to the job file instantly and cannot be lost.

Does it track where my trucks are?

Every crew appears on a live map. A geofence stamps the arrival time automatically when the crew reaches the address, so you know the job started because the app saw them arrive.

What does it cost?

Plans start at $40 a month, priced by truck: $40 for up to 2 trucks, $60 for 3 trucks, $80 for up to 5. More than 5 trucks, contact us. Everything is included. Prices are in Canadian dollars. See the full pricing.

Does it handle payroll for movers?

No — and we won't pretend it does. CrewFinding tracks every mover's hours per job and totals them into a timesheet, so when you run payroll you're working from exact tracked hours instead of paper or memory.

I book moves, but other crews run them. Does this work for me?

That's one of the main reasons it exists. Put the crew on CrewFinding and you watch the move from wherever you are: the arrival stamp, the signed BOL, the payment landing through the app instead of someone's pocket. You don't have to own the truck to see the whole job.
