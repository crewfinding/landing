Questions landscaping companies ask

Before you download it.

Does CrewFinding work for small landscaping companies?

That is exactly who it is built for: landscaping companies running two to five crews. It is priced by truck, from $40 a month, with everyone included.

Can it tell me which lawns make money?

Yes. Every yard gets its own clock, and the app shows each lawn's price minus the crew time it burned. A $45 yard that takes 1:04 of a three-man crew shows its loss the day it happens — not at the end of the season.

Does it track my crews between yards?

Every crew appears on a live map, and a geofence stamps the arrival at each property automatically. You can watch the whole route from home, the shop, or another city.

Can my crew prove a yard was done?

Yes. The crew snaps a photo of the finished yard and it files to that property's record with the time and the GPS stamp. When a client says you never came, you answer with a photo taken at 8:33 in their backyard.

Can clients sign seasonal contracts on a phone?

Yes. The client signs the season contract on your crew's phone at the door, and it files itself to the client's record instantly. It cannot be lost.

What does it cost?

Plans start at $40 a month, priced by truck: $40 for up to 2 trucks, $60 for 3 trucks, $80 for up to 5. More than 5 trucks, contact us. Everything is included. Prices are in Canadian dollars. See the full pricing.

Does it handle payroll for my crew?

No — and we won't pretend it does. CrewFinding tracks every worker's hours per yard and totals them into a timesheet, so when you run payroll you're working from exact tracked hours instead of paper or memory.
